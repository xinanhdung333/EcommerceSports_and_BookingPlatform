TOM TAT DU AN SPORTSHUB
Tai lieu dau vao de Claude viet bao cao do an

1. TONG QUAN DU AN

SportsHub la ung dung web co dinh huong chinh la thuong mai dien tu ban san pham/do the thao. He thong ho tro khach xem san pham, gio hang, dat mua, thanh toan, giao hang va theo doi don hang. Ngoai nghiep vu ban hang, du an con co phan he dat san bong va cac dich vu di kem.

README hien tai chu yeu mo ta he thong dat san bong, chua phan anh day du cac chuc nang thuong mai dien tu trong ma nguon. Khi viet bao cao, can lay website ban do the thao lam trong tam; gioi thieu dat san bong nhu mot phan he bo sung dac thu dang co trong ung dung.

2. NGHIEP VU VA CHUC NANG CHINH

2.1. Mua sam truc tuyen

- Khach hang xem danh sach san pham va chi tiet san pham.
- Tim kiem san pham theo ten; loc theo danh muc; sap xep theo ten, gia tang/giam hoac danh gia.
- San pham co cac thong tin nhu ten, mo ta, gia, hinh anh, so luong ton kho, danh muc, trang thai va danh gia.
- Ho tro khuyen mai/flash sale, tinh gia sau giam gia.
- Khach hang them san pham vao gio hang, cap nhat so luong, xoa san pham, mua ngay, thanh toan mot phan gio hang hoac toan bo gio hang.
- Ho tro luu dia chi giao hang, chon phuong thuc giao hang va ghi chu don hang.
- Ho tro voucher giam gia co dinh, giam theo phan tram hoac mien phi van chuyen; voucher co the co gia tri don toi thieu, muc giam toi da va ngay het han.
- Khach hang xem danh sach don hang, chi tiet don hang va lich su mua hang.
- He thong kiem tra ton kho khi tao don va tru ton kho khi don hang duoc xac nhan thanh toan.

Luu y ve cach dat ten trong ma nguon: san pham hien duoc luu qua model/ten bang Service/services va giao dien quan tri con co cho goi la “dich vu”. Day la cach dat ten ke thua tu phan he dat san. Trong bao cao thuong mai dien tu, co the dien giai cac doi tuong nay la san pham/hang hoa the thao. Khong nen khang dinh he thong co quan ly bien the SKU nhu size/mau neu khong co can cu trong du lieu hien tai.

2.2. Quy trinh don hang va thanh toan

Quy trinh tong quat:
Khach chon san pham -> them vao gio -> chon dia chi va phuong thuc giao hang -> ap dung voucher -> chon phuong thuc thanh toan -> he thong cap nhat don hang -> khach theo doi trang thai giao hang.

Phuong thuc thanh toan hien co trong ma nguon:

- Tien mat: he thong cap nhat trang thai thanh toan/don hang va tru ton kho trong giao dich co so du lieu.
- MoMo: co luong tao yeu cau thanh toan, chuyen khach sang cong thanh toan va xu ly return/IPN. Ma nguon kiem tra chu ky va so tien giao dich. Endpoint dang su dung la cong test/sandbox; can cau hinh thong tin ket noi moi co the su dung.
- Chuyen khoan ngan hang: he thong tao/thong tin QR chuyen khoan; webhook SePay tiep nhan giao dich va xac nhan thanh toan sau khi kiem tra chu ky HMAC, thoi gian, ma don va so tien.

Du lieu don hang, chi tiet don, thanh toan, dia chi giao hang va van chuyen duoc luu tach biet trong cac bang/model tuong ung. Trang thai thanh toan va don hang duoc cap nhat qua cac luong thanh toan.

2.3. Van chuyen va theo doi giao hang

- Quan tri phuong thuc giao hang, phu phi va nguong mien phi van chuyen.
- Tinh phi giao hang dua tren khoang cach tu cua hang den dia chi giao hang; co the dung phi co dinh neu khong lay duoc khoang cach.
- Chuyen dia chi thanh toa do de ho tro tinh phi va theo doi.
- Co trang theo doi giao hang voi ma theo doi, trang thai, cac moc tien trinh, toa do va tuyen duong hien thi tren ban do.
- Co ma nguon tich hop GHN cho du lieu dia chi va tao van don; tuy nhien cau hinh mac dinh cua GHN la demo/mo phong. Viec tao van don that can duoc cau hinh/bat rieng. Khong nen mo ta la he thong dang san sang giao hang production neu chua xac nhan cau hinh thuc te.
- Co vai tro shipper de xem don giao va cap nhat trang thai van chuyen.

2.4. Phan he dat san bong

Day la phan he bo sung, song song voi cua hang truc tuyen:

- Xem danh sach san bong, thong tin va gia thue theo gio.
- Xem lich san va dat san theo ngay, gio bat dau, gio ket thuc.
- Kiem tra trung khung gio va tinh gia dat san dua tren thoi luong.
- Chon san pham/dich vu di kem khi dat san.
- Xem lich su booking, tim kiem, loc theo trang thai, xem chi tiet va huy booking theo dieu kien.
- Theo doi trang thai booking, gom cac trang thai cho xu ly, da xac nhan, dang dien ra, hoan tat, het han hoac da huy tuy theo luong.
- Thanh toan booking bang tien mat, MoMo hoac chuyen khoan.

2.5. Tai khoan, quan tri va cham soc khach hang

- Dang ky, dang nhap, dang xuat, cap nhat ho so, doi/quen mat khau.
- Ho tro dang nhap Google/Facebook qua Laravel Socialite neu da cau hinh Client ID va Client Secret.
- Phan quyen theo vai tro: khach, nguoi dung, admin, boss va shipper.
- Admin/boss quan ly san pham, danh muc, ton kho, don hang, booking, tai khoan, voucher, chinh sach/gia, van chuyen, feedback va thong ke.
- Co hoa don PDF cho don hang va booking.
- Khach hang gui feedback/danh gia; quan tri co the quan ly va phan hoi.
- Chat ho tro giua khach va admin; backend luu hoi thoai/tin nhan, co ho tro dinh kem tep va broadcast qua WebSocket.
- Chatbot co the tra loi mot so cau hoi san pham dua tren database, thu khop intent/keyword va response da cau hinh; neu khong khop thi co the goi Gemini khi da cau hinh API key.

Luu y: trang wishlist hien co route tra ve view, nhung chua du can cu de mo ta day la tinh nang yeu thich hoan thien.

3. CONG NGHE VA KIEN TRUC

- Backend: PHP 8.2 tro len, Laravel Framework 12.
- Kien truc web: Laravel MVC, controller, Eloquent ORM, middleware, request validation, migration va Blade views.
- Giao dien: Blade render phia server; Bootstrap 5 va Bootstrap Icons duoc nap tu CDN; Vite quan ly/build tai nguyen CSS/JavaScript. CSS co Tailwind directives; JavaScript su dung Axios va cac thu vien duoc khai bao trong package.json.
- Co so du lieu: Laravel cau hinh SQLite lam ket noi mac dinh neu moi truong khong ghi de; Docker Compose khai bao MySQL 8. Co the mo ta ung dung ho tro SQLite/MySQL va moi truong Docker dinh huong MySQL.
- API: REST API phien ban /api/v1. Laravel Sanctum cap token cho xac thuc API. API hien co cac luong xac thuc, lay sanh sach san/san pham va dat san; chua thay API checkout/gio hang thuong mai dien tu rieng.
- Xu ly nen: co migration va cau hinh queue/job trong Laravel.
- Xuat hoa don: DomPDF.
- Moi truong Docker: PHP-FPM, Nginx, MySQL, Redis, Node/Vite va WebSocket server.
- WebSocket server rieng duoc viet bang Node.js va goi thu vien ws.

4. DU LIEU NGHIEP VU CHINH

Mot so entity/bang quan trong:

- users: tai khoan, thong tin ca nhan va role.
- categories: danh muc san pham/dich vu.
- services: du lieu san pham/hang hoa theo cach dat ten hien tai cua code, gom gia, ton kho, mo ta, hinh anh, danh muc va danh gia.
- carts, cart_items: gio hang va cac mat hang trong gio.
- orders, order_items: don hang va chi tiet don hang.
- payments: thanh toan don hang.
- user_addresses: dia chi nguoi dung, co the luu thong tin ma dia chi GHN va toa do.
- order_shipments, shipping_statuses, order_shipping_status_histories: thong tin va lich su giao van.
- vouchers, shipping_methods, settings: ma giam gia, phuong thuc van chuyen va cau hinh cua hang.
- fields: san bong.
- bookings, booking_services, booking_payments: booking, dich vu/san pham di kem va thanh toan booking.
- feedbacks: danh gia/phan hoi.
- conversations, messages: chat ho tro.
- chatbot_intents, chatbot_keywords, chatbot_responses, chatbot_logs: cau hinh va nhat ky chatbot.
- invoices, activity_logs: hoa don va lich su hoat dong.

5. CAC TICH HOP BEN NGOAI

MoMo:
- Thanh toan cho don mua hang va booking.
- Ma nguon co ky du lieu, redirect, return/IPN va xu ly trang thai.
- Dang cau hinh goi endpoint test/sandbox; can bien moi truong hop le de ket noi.

Ngan hang va SePay:
- Hien thi thong tin/QR chuyen khoan.
- SePay webhook nhan thong bao tien vao, xac thuc HMAC-SHA256 va doi chieu ma don/so tien truoc khi cap nhat thanh toan.

GHN:
- Lay du lieu tinh/thanh pho, quan/huyen, phuong/xa va ho tro tao/theo doi van don.
- Code co cac che do demo/test; mac dinh la mo phong, khong duoc tuyen bo rang da van hanh giao hang that.

OpenRouteService:
- Tinh khoang cach duong di va lay diem tuyen duong.
- Duoc dung trong logic tinh phi van chuyen/theo doi.
- Can ORS API key; ket qua duoc cache de han che goi dich vu lap lai.

OpenStreetMap Nominatim:
- Geocoding dia chi thanh toa do.
- Duoc su dung de phuc vu tinh phi va tuyen duong.

Google va Facebook OAuth:
- Dang nhap mang xa hoi qua Socialite.
- Chi hoat dong khi co Client ID/Secret va redirect URI phu hop.

Gemini:
- Chatbot goi Gemini API neu da cau hinh API key.
- La luong tuy chon; chatbot van co tra loi dua tren database va rule/intent.

WebSocket:
- Node.js server phat tin nhan chat theo conversation.
- Co endpoint health va HTTP broadcast noi bo tu Laravel.

DomPDF:
- Tao file hoa don PDF.

6. PHAM VI API VA KIEM THU

- API /api/v1 co dang ky/dang nhap, lay thong tin tai khoan, dang xuat, danh sach/chi tiet san bong, danh sach/chi tiet san pham, kiem tra lich trong va CRUD booking co xac thuc Sanctum.
- API booking co request validation, resource response va kiem tra quyen so huu booking.
- Co feature tests cho API booking, danh sach san/san pham, dang nhap token va kiem tra trung lich.
- Co cac test auth, profile, reset password va mot so browser test.
- Khong nen khang dinh API co day du chuc nang mua hang, gio hang hoac checkout neu chua bo sung API tuong ung.

7. CAC DIEM CAN TRINH BAY CHINH XAC TRONG BAO CAO

1. Xac dinh de tai la website thuong mai dien tu ban do the thao; dat san bong la phan he mo rong.
2. Mo ta ro luong san pham, gio hang, voucher, thanh toan, ton kho, don hang va giao van.
3. Giai thich ten ky thuat Service/services trong code dang duoc dung cho du lieu san pham.
4. Phan biet ma nguon tich hop voi dich vu da duoc cau hinh/chay that: MoMo test, GHN mac dinh demo, Gemini/OAuth can API credentials.
5. API hien tap trung vao booking va catalog co ban; khong tuyen bo co API thanh toan/gio hang neu khong co bang chung.
6. Khong tu them cac tinh nang chua co bang chung nhu app di dong, quan ly size/mau san pham, ERP, quan ly kho nhieu chi nhanh hoac tich hop giao hang production.
7. Khi noi ve SQLite/MySQL, ghi dung la cau hinh mac dinh cua Laravel la SQLite neu khong override, trong khi Docker Compose cung cap MySQL 8.


CHUONG 3: XAY DUNG VA TRIEN KHAI HE THONG

Muc tieu cua chuong:
Mo ta cach giao dien, backend va cac chuc nang chinh cua SportsHub duoc hien thuc hoa trong ma nguon. Trong toan bo chuong, lay nghiep vu thuong mai dien tu ban do the thao lam trong tam; trinh bay dat san bong la phan he bo sung. Phan biet tinh nang da co trong code voi cac dich vu can cau hinh moi hoat dong day du.

3.1. Xay dung giao dien (Frontend)

3.1.1. Cong nghe va cach to chuc

- Giao dien web duoc render bang Laravel Blade; cac trang duoc chia thanh layout, partial, component va view theo nhom chuc nang.
- Bootstrap 5 va Bootstrap Icons duoc nap tu CDN trong giao dien; CSS tu du an nam trong public/assets va resources/css.
- Vite duoc cau hinh build resources/css/app.css va resources/js/app.js. resources/js/bootstrap.js khoi tao Axios voi header XMLHttpRequest.
- Mot so thao tac su dung request AJAX/JSON, vi du cap nhat gio hang, lay tinh trang giao hang va chat. Cac trang con lai su dung form HTML/POST va redirect theo cach xu ly cua Laravel.
- Giao dien co cac nhom khu vuc rieng cho khach/nguoi mua, admin, boss va shipper. Quyen truy cap route duoc gioi han bang middleware auth va role.
- Giao dien nguoi dung co cac trang san pham, chi tiet san pham, gio hang, thanh toan, don hang, dia chi, booking, ho so, feedback va chat.
- Giao dien quan tri co cac trang quan ly san pham/danh muc, don hang, booking, tai khoan, voucher, phuong thuc giao hang, feedback, thong ke va hoi thoai chat.

3.1.2. Cac man hinh va thanh phan chinh

1. Trang danh sach san pham:
   Hien thi luoi san pham, hinh anh, ten, danh muc, gia, khuyen mai va thong tin danh gia. Ho tro tim theo ten, loc danh muc, sap xep theo gia/ten/danh gia va lien ket den chi tiet san pham. View tham khao: resources/views/user/services.blade.php.

2. Trang chi tiet san pham:
   Trinh bay thong tin san pham, gia va tinh trang hang; cho phep chon so luong va them vao gio/mua ngay. Co the co danh gia/feedback lien quan. View tham khao: resources/views/user/service-detail.blade.php va route user.serviceDetail; neu ten view thuc te khac, can doi chieu PagesController::serviceDetail.

3. Gio hang:
   Hien thi cac mat hang da chon, don gia, so luong va thanh tien; ho tro chon mat hang de checkout, tang/giam so luong va xoa mat hang. Giao dien co tab lich su mua. View tham khao: resources/views/user/cart.blade.php.

4. Thanh toan:
   Hien thi dia chi giao hang, chon/cap nhat dia chi, thong tin tinh/quan/phuong, phi giao hang, phuong thuc van chuyen, voucher va phuong thuc thanh toan. Ho tro MoMo, tien mat va chuyen khoan theo route/controller. View tham khao: resources/views/user/payment-method.blade.php va cac view checkout.

5. Don hang va theo doi giao hang:
   Nguoi mua xem don hang, trang thai, chi tiet va trang tracking. Trang tracking trinh bay nha van chuyen, ma van don, dia chi, trang thai va ban do/tuyen duong neu du lieu duoc cung cap. View tham khao: resources/views/user/orders.blade.php, resources/views/user/order-detail.blade.php va resources/views/user/order-tracking.blade.php.

6. Quan tri:
   Cac trang bang du lieu/form cho admin va boss quan ly san pham, danh muc, don hang, booking, tai khoan va cau hinh. View tham khao: resources/views/admin/manage-services.blade.php, resources/views/admin/manage-orders.blade.php va cac view tuong ung trong resources/views/boss.

7. Dat san va ho tro:
   Cac view rieng cho danh sach san, lich san, booking, thanh toan booking, feedback, chat va chatbot. Day la chuc nang bo sung so voi luong ban hang.

3.1.3. Thiet ke trai nghiem va xu ly giao dien

- Giao dien danh sach san pham uu tien hien thi thong tin can thiet de nguoi mua so sanh va di den trang chi tiet.
- Gio hang va thanh toan tach thanh cac buoc de nguoi mua kiem tra mat hang, so luong, dia chi, chi phi va phuong thuc tra tien.
- Form du lieu duoc xu ly qua validation phia server; thong bao thanh cong/loi duoc hien thi qua session flash message hoac JSON response.
- Cac trang co phan bo cuc responsive Bootstrap va mot so CSS rieng cho man hinh nho.
- Mot so phan nhu cap nhat gio hang va tracking co the cap nhat du lieu ma khong can tai lai toan bo trang; can chup man hinh tu trinh duyet khi app dang chay de minh hoa dung thuc te.

3.2. Lap trinh backend (PHP + co so du lieu)

3.2.1. To chuc backend

- Laravel 12 ap dung mo hinh MVC: route tiep nhan request, controller dieu phoi nghiep vu, model Eloquent thao tac du lieu, Blade render trang web.
- routes/web.php khai bao cac route web cho mua sam, gio hang, thanh toan, booking, admin, boss va shipper.
- routes/api.php khai bao API /api/v1; cac route can dang nhap dung auth:sanctum.
- Middleware auth va role kiem soat dang nhap/quyen. Cac route webhook thanh toan duoc cau hinh de tiep nhan callback tu ben ngoai.
- Form request/controller validation kiem tra du lieu dau vao; quan he Eloquent mo ta lien ket giua user, order, order item, payment, service, booking va field.
- Database migrations mo ta cau truc bang; transaction duoc dung o mot so thao tac can cap nhat dong bo, nhu tao don va tru ton kho khi xac nhan thanh toan.

3.2.2. Luong backend mua hang va tao don

1. Backend lay danh sach san pham tu services, lien ket category va tinh gia khuyen mai neu co rule dang hieu luc.
2. Khi them san pham vao gio, he thong tao/lay gio hang cua user va cap nhat cart_items; kiem tra so luong yeu cau voi ton kho.
3. Khi checkout, backend doc cac mat hang thuoc gio cua user, tinh gia dua tren du lieu trong database va kiem tra ton kho. Don hang va cac order_items duoc tao voi trang thai cho xu ly/thanh toan.
4. Backend tinh tong tien, phi giao hang va voucher; du lieu thanh toan duoc tao/cap nhat trong payments.
5. Sau khi thanh toan duoc chap nhan, backend cap nhat trang thai payment/order/item, tru ton kho va xoa cac mat hang tuong ung khoi gio hang theo luong xu ly.
6. Giao dich database giup cac thay doi lien quan duoc thuc hien cung nhau trong mot so luong thanh toan, tranh cap nhat mot phan neu co loi.
7. Nguoi mua truy van danh sach/chi tiet don cua minh; controller kiem tra quyen so huu truoc khi hien thi cac thao tac nhay cam.

Can luu y: chi tiet tinh toan va thoi diem cap nhat trang thai co the khac nhau theo phuong thuc thanh toan. Khi viet bao cao, mo ta theo luong trong code, khong tu khang dinh tat ca cong thanh toan da duoc kiem thu tren production.

3.2.3. Xu ly thanh toan

- Tien mat: backend cap nhat payment thanh cong va order sang trang thai xac nhan; ton kho duoc tru trong transaction.
- MoMo: backend tao request den endpoint test-payment.momo.vn, tao chu ky theo cau hinh, luu ma don MoMo, sau do xu ly return/IPN. Khi nhan ket qua, code kiem tra chu ky, resultCode va so tien truoc khi cap nhat trang thai.
- Chuyen khoan: man hinh thanh toan tao huong dan/QR dua tren thong tin tai khoan cau hinh. Endpoint SePay nhan webhook; backend doc raw body, kiem tra HMAC-SHA256 va timestamp trong khoang cho phep, phan tich ma order/booking trong noi dung, doi chieu so tien va cap nhat giao dich.
- Tuyet doi khong dua secret/key that vao bao cao, screenshot, commit hoac prompt cho cong cu ben ngoai.
- Khong co bang chung ve VNPay trong routes/controller/config da kiem tra. Neu de cuong yeu cau ke VNPay, phai ghi ro “chua trien khai/chua xac nhan trong ma nguon” hoac de xuat la huong phat trien; khong viet VNPay nhu tinh nang da hoan thanh.

3.2.4. Xu ly van chuyen

- Dia chi giao hang duoc luu lien ket voi user va order; co cac thong tin hanh chinh GHN va/toa do neu da duoc cap nhat.
- ShippingFeeController va OpenRouteService tinh phi theo toa do/khoang cach, nguong mien phi; khi thieu toa do hoac dich vu khong tra duoc ket qua co the dung phi co dinh theo cau hinh.
- ShippingService tao ban ghi van chuyen, ma tracking, diem lay/giao va cac diem tuyen duong; neu khong lay duoc tuyen thuc co the dung tuyen duong demo.
- GHNService co cac ham lay dia chi, tao don va cap nhat trang thai demo. Cac credential va mode GHN can duoc cau hinh rieng.

3.2.5. Mo hinh du lieu phuc vu thuong mai dien tu

- User co nhieu Order va UserAddress.
- Order co nhieu OrderItem; moi OrderItem tham chieu mot Service/san pham, luu so luong, gia va thong tin gia goc/giam gia tai thoi diem mua.
- Cart co nhieu CartItem; CartItem tham chieu Service/san pham va so luong.
- Order co Payment va thong tin van chuyen; voucher va phi ship duoc ghi tren don de tinh tong phai tra.
- Service thuoc Category va co the co feedback/danh gia.
- Cac bang nay duoc tao/cap nhat qua Laravel migrations; SQLite duoc cau hinh cho testing, MySQL duoc cung cap trong Docker Compose.

3.3. Cac chuc nang chinh da hoan thien trong ma nguon

Trong bao cao, “da hoan thien” o day chi co nghia la co luong code/giao dien tuong ung trong repository; khong dong nghia da duoc kiem thu chap nhan day du tren moi moi truong.

3.3.1. Tai khoan va phan quyen

- Dang ky, dang nhap, dang xuat, ho so, cap nhat mat khau va quy trinh quen/tao lai mat khau.
- Co code dang nhap Google/Facebook qua Socialite, phu thuoc vao credentials.
- Route tach theo user/admin/boss/shipper va co middleware bao ve.

3.3.2. Danh muc va san pham

- Xem, tim kiem, loc va sap xep danh sach san pham.

4.3. Ket qua kiem thu

4.3.1. Ket qua chay tu dong da quan sat

Lenh da chay: `php artisan test`
Ngay chay theo phien lam viec: 2026-10-08.
Ket qua: 33 tests tong cong; 2 passed, 31 failed; 2 assertions; thoi gian khoang 92 giay.

Nguyen nhan loi chung hien thi trong output:
Migration `2026_09_19_000003_add_shipper_role_to_users_table.php` chay SQL theo cu phap MySQL:
`ALTER TABLE users MODIFY role ENUM('user', 'admin', 'boss', 'shipper') NOT NULL DEFAULT 'user'`
Bo test dang cau hinh SQLite in-memory trong phpunit.xml, SQLite khong ho tro cu phap `ALTER TABLE ... MODIFY ... ENUM` nay. Loi xuat hien trong qua trinh setup database/test, truoc khi nhieu test feature co the kiem tra nghiep vu.

- Tests\Unit\ExampleTest: 1 test pass.

31 test fail khong duoc dien giai thanh 31 loi nghiep vu rieng biet: phan lon bi chan boi loi tuong thich migration SQLite neu cung mot exception lap lai. Can xu ly migration de phu hop SQLite hoac chay test tren MySQL rieng roi chay lai suite; chi sau do moi ket luan pass/fail ve nghiep vu. Khong sua migration trong yeu cau tao tai lieu nay.
